# Penns

Most notes apps are quiet archives of good intentions. Lists get written and never read again. Thoughts get saved so they can be safely forgotten.

Penns is the opposite. There is one note, and only one. It sits in the corner of your screen and waits for you to look at it. The note isn't a record of your past. It's a promise to your future self.

A note should live only as long as it matters. When it has done its job, you archive it, and the page is blank again. You haven't lost anything. You've cleared space. Some notes are given a lifespan, and some mornings begin empty on purpose, because tomorrow deserves its own page.

Holding one thing is lighter than holding everything.
