# Penns

**One note. Always in sight. Gone when it's done.**

Most notes apps turn into archives of good intentions. You write a list, and you never see it again. Penns works the other way round: there's exactly one note, and it stays where you'll see it. It sits in your Mac's menu bar, on your iPhone's Lock Screen and on your wrist.

That's the whole idea, and it's enough. You don't file anything. You don't search for anything. You open Penns, write what matters today, and get on with it.

## What it's good at

- **Being there.** A click in the menu bar, a glance at the Lock Screen, a look at your watch. The note is ready before you've finished thinking.
- **Getting things done.** Type `- ` and you have a checklist. Tick things off from the widget, the Lock Screen or the watch without opening the app. When everything is done, Penns offers to archive the note.
- **Letting go.** Give a note a lifespan (today, this week, until a time you choose), or start every morning with an empty page. Archived notes aren't deleted. They wait quietly in case you need them.
- **Staying simple.** Lists, headings, bold and the like, in the same way Apple Notes does them. Nothing else to learn.

## Where it lives

- **Mac:** in the menu bar, as a desktop widget, and in the Services menu of every app.
- **iPhone and iPad:** full screen, as Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and as a Live Activity across the whole Lock Screen.
- **Apple Watch:** as a complication, in the Smart Stack, with dictation for new lines.
- **Everywhere:** Shortcuts, Focus filters and the share sheet.

Penns is free. No subscription, no account, no ads.

Holding one thing is lighter than holding everything.
